Shared materials (2026-11-07): the 30 `frontier_*.glb` models no longer embed their textures. The default exporter includes the refined ATP sources and the newer frontier source above. Coordinates are Godot east/up/south, converted to Blender east/north/up. Ground is local height zero, and building fronts face Godot +Z. Authoring budgets prioritize the standard isometric camera, with simple hidden closures. Existing transport and seasonal vegetation are reused at their current presentation scales. The base models use deterministic material bakes; the ATP revision uses separately prepared ImageGen surfaces.
