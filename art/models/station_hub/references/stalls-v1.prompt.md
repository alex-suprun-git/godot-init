# Market stalls design proposal v1

Built-in ImageGen. Separate modular modeling projections; not approved for production.

Create a landscape 3D MODELING REFERENCE SHEET for late-1990s post-Soviet clothing-market stalls beside a provincial railway station. Buildable modular family of THREE related stall types, not a whole market painting. A: simple 3 m wide x 2.5 m deep stall with 2.2 m eaves and 2.8 m ridge, thin painted square steel-tube frame and slightly sagging gabled canvas awning, red/cream stripes. B: same base module with faded blue waterproof tarp and one tied-up side curtain. C: two joined 3 m modules with muted ochre/green canvas, selling folded clothes and bags. A should dominate: show its matching true ORTHOGRAPHIC FRONT, BACK, SIDE, TOP with identical frame, seams, proportions and merchandise across views, plus a small three-quarter preview. In a separate lower band show B and C as clear three-quarter variants and a simple bare-frame assembly sketch. Plain light grey studio background, subtle grid, same metre scale, small clear English panel labels. No invented tiny dimension strings. Goods are simple hanging jackets, shirts, folded textile stacks, cardboard boxes and zipped striped trader bags; model-ready readable volumes with limited clutter. Slightly sun-faded fabric, sewn repairs, modest dust, maintained working stalls, August 1997. Muted but lively colors, painterly material grain on crisp believable 3D geometry, no toy proportions, no cartoon outlines, no pixel art. Realistic slender tubular construction and guy ties, fabric thickness, open customer front, usable counter around 0.9 m high. Optional one small detached neutral 1.75 m human scale silhouette, secondary. No huge gazebo pillars, tiled permanent shop, modern popup festival tent, medieval bazaar, ruined fabric, electric string lights, crowd, surrounding buildings or complete market layout. These are production reference projections for replacing every painted stall with actual 3D assets.
