# Station design proposal v1

Built-in ImageGen. User requested separate modeling projections and a lived-in 1970s–1980s station in 1997. Not approved for production.

Create a landscape architectural MODELING REFERENCE SHEET for one original provincial railway station built in the USSR in the 1970s–1980s, seen in August 1997 in a fictional Slavic post-Soviet border trading city. This is a design proposal for a full 3D strategy-game asset, not the earlier yellow neoclassical station. Moderate-sized civic building: suggested footprint 36 m long x 14 m deep, tall single-storey public hall about 6 m to roof, low service wing. Late-Soviet modernism: long horizontal composition, broad rectangular glazed entrance hall, flat roof, projecting concrete entrance canopy, pale warm precast concrete or limestone-faced panels, muted olive/blue-green metal window frames, dark red-brown ceramic plinth, restrained geometric ceramic mosaic panel, simple rooftop clock. Station street facade and platform facade must both have recognizable entrances and be different functional elevations of THE SAME BUILDING. Show true orthographic STREET FRONT, PLATFORM REAR, LEFT END, RIGHT END and ROOF PLAN with matching geometry and same scale; one smaller three-quarter shaded overview. Keep roof and sides fully visible, no cropped panels. Neutral light-grey background, subtle construction grid, clear panel spacing. Small title and view labels in English; dimensions are design targets, not surveyed data. A few lived-in 1997 additions: modest painted Cyrillic station sign ВОКЗАЛ, a beer/snack counter occupying one end bay, metal advertising frames, pasted travel and clothing-market ads, a restrained currency-exchange sign ОБМЕН, bench and rubbish bin as separate small prop callouts. Some mismatched replacement glazing and restrained rain streaks; functioning maintained public building with everyday wear, no abandonment, no ruin, no massive rust, no rubble. No modern LED advertising, contemporary logos, luxury terminal, Stalinist columns, pediment, green pitched roof or oversized shopping mall. Architectural game-art presentation: believable human proportions, crisp readable forms, materials with subtle painterly grain, softened physical edges, suitable for the existing grounded stylized isometric city. No dramatic lighting, no people crowd, no trains, no streetscape or whole plaza. This sheet will set the material family for a matching bus-station ensemble.
