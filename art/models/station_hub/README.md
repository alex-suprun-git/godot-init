## First 3D implementation — 2026-11-03

- Source: `build_assets.py` and editable `station_hub.blend`, built with installed Blender 5.2.3 in a separate background process because live MCP was unavailable. Each asset has its own named collection. Build/export are rerunnable; source textures are packed. Export explicitly selects only the current asset and active scene.
- Eight exports under `init/assets/models/station_hub/`: station, three stall variants, bus shelter, kiosk, bench and lamp. Binary inspection confirmed one scene/mesh per GLB and only the intended embedded images. `export_report.json` records relative paths and geometry counts: station about 6k triangles, each stall 484. These are first-pass models, not final art approval.
- `station_hub.gd` replaces the combined painted lot while keeping the `market` event ID. Station walls are 36 x 14 m, hall roof about 6 m above the raised floor; floor y=1.27 meets the platform. The market has 56 actual 3 x 2.5 m stall modules in four rows. Separate shelters/kiosks, existing LiAZ buses, cars/taxis, benches, bins and lamps complete the initial square. No hidden interiors or elaborate unseen facades were built.
- The shortened platform is 100 x 8.75 m (x=-139..-39, z=384..392.75), predominantly left/front of the station and ending before the bridge. The 82.286 m ER2 fits with centre x=-89, leaving about 8.86 m at each end. This is a review pose; normal trains continue their existing shuttles without passenger stops.
- The forecourt's two throats connect to south street at x=-130 and -46, away from the overpass ramp. Entry/link/exit are actual graph roads; a city traffic itinerary now drives through them. Static bus bays and parking are off the central through lane. A graphical run advanced normal traffic for 30 seconds without script/route errors. This does not certify collision-free swept turning envelopes or implement bus boarding.
- User feedback identified overlapping/dotted automatic zebras on the internal roads. Those roads are excluded from automatic crosswalk generation on both participating arms. One deliberately placed solid-bar crossing connects the station approach to the shelter island; surrounding city crossings remain automatic.
- After the user rejected the shared pale tiled base, `station_surfaces.gd` / `station_ground.gdshader` now distinguish three surfaces: grey repaired asphalt on the platform, restrained 0.6 m concrete paving on pedestrian areas, and ground-level worn asphalt with dusty aisles/broken grass edges beneath stalls. The market's raised paving plinth is removed and stall feet lowered to the actual surface. Existing asphalt grain is reused; this is shader/geometry work, with no new generated raster assets.
- The platform has instanced metre-long coping stones, two-metre concrete facing panels with physical joints, subdued safety paint, small back-edge drainage grates and a narrow ballast shoulder. The square has explicit low kerbs with openings at the pedestrian crossing, a narrower boarding island, and asphalt taxi pockets separated from the station's pedestrian apron. These appearance revisions are implemented and await user review; they are not an approved new art standard.
- `station_crowd.gd` adds 66 local ambient people using existing calibrated pedestrian sprites: 40 shoppers/walkers in the market, 10 at shelters, 8 at the station approach and 8 on the platform. Standing groups and slow walking/pause routes are confined to pedestrian areas and behind the platform safety line. An independent RNG keeps simulation residents/requests and the global ambient pool unchanged; only a quarter of this local pool remains visible at night. Actual game capture was inspected with the crowd present, without script errors.
- The bridge/parking check in the same capture reports all six separately registered structural nodes and zero parked vehicles overlapping their full bridge/ramp footprints. Static hub buses/cars use the same registry-based parking guard as street parking. Three eastern embankment spans are individually registered too; the railway route still takes its heights from the embankment and road users stay underneath those rail spans.
- Godot 4.7.2 import and focused graphical capture completed with exit 0, without script/shader errors in the final `hub-surfaces-final.log`. Actual standard-isometric overview and detail were inspected, including the empty platform to expose its surface/facing.
