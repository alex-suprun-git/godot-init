## Original prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a landscape 3D modeling reference sheet for a freight train in a fictional Slavic post-Soviet provincial city in 1997. This is a proposed design for user review, not an engineering blueprint.
Subject: ONE single-section diesel freight locomotive with an M62-inspired late-Soviet silhouette, hauling EXACTLY EIGHT identical four-axle container flat wagons. Each wagon carries ONE standard-height 40-foot corrugated shipping container, single stacked. One locomotive plus eight wagons total. No passenger cars.
The locomotive should have a convincing long heavy rectangular body, two end cabs, modestly rounded cab roof transitions, broad front windows, circular lamps, side ventilation grilles, two three-axle bogies, coherent fuel tank and underframe, central railway couplers. Choose one coherent original interpretation and keep it identical across views.
Composition: large clean modeling turnaround sheet on plain warm light grey background. Upper half: large locomotive strict side elevation plus front elevation and elevated three-quarter view. Middle: large single loaded flat wagon side elevation and elevated three-quarter view, with small isolated container view showing corrugation and end doors. Bottom: a small uninterrupted strict side profile of the COMPLETE train, locomotive at left followed by exactly eight loaded platforms, all fully visible with clear coupling gaps. Keep the bottom overview subordinate so detailed reference views remain large. Consistent geometry and proportions between views. Orthographic side/front views; elevated three-quarter approximates the game's distant city-map camera.
Style: solid readable simplified 3D game asset, moderately stylized realistic proportions, restrained matte materials, clean silhouettes, compatible with a maintained 1990s city and modest low-detail vehicles. Clear bogies, wheel contact and couplers. Avoid microscopic bolts and noisy surface detail.
Color: locomotive muted desaturated green with a restrained cream band as a tentative proposal; all eight containers the SAME neutral grey, all wagon frames the SAME dark grey. Color variants will be designed later; keep container panels, wagon frame, wheel assemblies and locomotive livery visually separable for future independent recoloring. Intact paint and only light service grime.
Lighting: neutral soft studio daylight, readable roof shapes and undercarriage, no dramatic shadows.
Avoid: double-section locomotive, pantographs, overhead wires, steam engines, futuristic streamlining, US-style hood locomotive, double-stack containers, extra wagons, extra locomotives in the full-train overview, rainbow colors, branding, flags, political symbols, heavy rust, decay, scenery, people, fake technical dimensions, decorative diagrams, watermark. Small simple view labels allowed.
