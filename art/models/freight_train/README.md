# Freight locomotive — M62-inspired game interpretation

Created 2026-11-02. The user approved the locomotive and full eight-wagon formation with three container paints, then requested swapping tracks and ambient shuttle movement. Freight now uses the outer eastern track (x=420), ER2 the inner (x=410). Operating behavior and timing are documented under Ambient trains in the project `README.md`.

## Source, scale and review

- `locomotive.blend`: editable `M62_Studio` scene, one authored mesh; `locomotive_blockout.blend` preserves the cheap silhouette checkpoint. Two end cabs, crowned roof, six axles, fuel tank, side grilles, roof fans, lamps, steps and handrails. Opaque glass; no interior, animation or generated raster textures.
- `build_locomotive.py` reuses `Geometry`, material creation and studio helpers from `../public_transport/er2/build_er2.py`; the diesel body and equipment are new geometry. `export_locomotive.py` exports only the selected locomotive from its active scene and reimports the GLB for measurements. `preview_locomotive.py` creates a live comparison using the actual locomotive, ER2 head/motor, Volga and KamAZ exports; the optional comparison copy is ignored under `.local/freight-train/`.
- Game asset: `init/assets/models/vehicles/m62_locomotive.glb`. Preserve its `er2_material_import.gd` import hook for linear vertex colors. Forward is Blender -Y / Godot +Z; wheel bottoms are at zero. Three portable material surfaces, 9,632 triangles against a 14k budget for one map locomotive; no embedded images. Actual binary measurements: `export_report.json`.
- `container_wagon.blend` contains one reusable four-axle platform with one standard-height 40 ft container; `wagon_blockout.blend` preserves its silhouette stage. Sources: `build_wagon.py`, `export_wagon.py`, `preview_wagon.py`. The independent cargo-paint surface supports per-instance tinting while frame, wheels, locking bars and corner openings keep their materials. `container_flat_wagon.glb` has 4,704 triangles against a 5,500 budget, three surfaces and no images; its import uses the same vertex-color hook. Actual bounds and formation measurements: `wagon_export_report.json`.
- `assemble_freight.py` saves `freight_train.blend`, an editable assembly of the actual exports: one locomotive and exactly eight linked wagon meshes. `init/scripts/map/freight_train.gd` builds the same formation in Godot, with shared meshes and three cached cargo paints: muted brick `985a49`, blue-grey `526c7b`, olive `6b7450`. Order behind the locomotive: brick, blue, olive, brick, olive, blue, brick, blue. Coupler-to-coupler wagon length is 15.146315 source m / 14.903696 map m; complete formation is 137.120 m and 47,264 triangles. Container nominal dimensions are 12.192 × 2.438 × 2.591 m before presentation factors; its bottom sits at 1.400 m above wheel contact.
- Nominal construction targets for this fictional interpretation are 17.550 m over couplers, 2.950 m body width and 4.493 m maximum height; the main roof is 4.400 m. These are construction anchors, not a certified subtype reconstruction. The generated sheet is not a dimensioned blueprint. Like the existing ER2 and trucks, the mesh uses the measured Volga L/W/H presentation factors from the ER2 record. Actual exported bounds including fittings are 18.1818 × 3.5360 × 4.4930 m, before the common railway map factor 0.983982. The displayed locomotive is about 17.891 × 3.479 × 4.421 m. The truck comparison additionally uses its existing approved 0.80 factor.
- User-confirmed comparison rule: shorter and narrower than an ER2 car, with a slightly higher main roof but below its pantograph. Do not scale the locomotive to fill a passenger-car footprint. The actual ER2 head export is 20.9065 m long, 4.0760 m wide including fittings; its nominal roof is 4.253 m, and the motor-car pantograph reaches 5.486 m before the common map factor.
- Inspected: live Blender locomotive blockout side/front/three-quarter, rendered wagon blockout, Godot comparison beside actual ER2/Volga/KamAZ, and actual city-map overview/close frames of the full eight-wagon formation. Latest map and full-formation viewer launches completed without script or rendering errors on installed Godot 4.7.2. No automated tests were added or run. Local frames/logs: `.local/freight-train/`; they are not repository review assets. Blender MCP became unavailable after the locomotive review, so wagon build/export/assembly used installed Blender 5.2.3 in background mode.
- Operation: `DistrictBuilder` creates `railway_traffic.gd`; `railway_route.gd` shares its rounded southern bend with the drawn tracks and samples the embankment/bridge heights. Each car follows the route using its bogie span, preserving the rear ER2 cab orientation and leading locomotive on return trips. Formations are reused, hidden and reversed only beyond opaque haze. `DistrictLayout` assigns tracks; no generated model or export changed for motion. Stations, wheel animation, loading and operating signals remain outside this ambient feature.
- Motion review: `--autoplay --only=trains --shots=<absolute directory>` advances the real scheduler through two completed trips per train and saves startup, bend, mist and simultaneous-pass frames plus `trains-review.json`. Each dedicated track retains exactly one formation; simultaneous travel is allowed and each completed trip has a 10 s hidden wait. `capture_map.gd` selects a scheduled freight pass for the older asset-only framing instead of injecting stock. Current evidence and validation are recorded in the PR; frames/logs remain local under `.local/railway-motion/`.

Rebuild stages in live Blender with `runpy.run_path(<absolute script path>, run_name='__main__')`: build, export, preview. Set `LOCOMOTIVE_BLOCKOUT=1` in the process environment only for the blockout build. Scripts preserve unrelated live scenes and write only the authored asset scene to the source `.blend`.

Wagon stages can also run with `Blender --background --factory-startup --python <absolute script path>`: `build_wagon.py`, `export_wagon.py`, then `assemble_freight.py`. For the blockout, load `build_wagon.py` with `runpy.run_path(..., run_name='wagon_helpers')` and call its `build(blockout=True)`; render it using `preview_wagon.py -- --blockout`.

Godot orbit viewer: `res://scenes/dev/locomotive_3d_demo.tscn` (based on the ER2 viewer). Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom; buttons switch between comparison, locomotive alone and the full eight-wagon formation. Optional `-- --solo` or `-- --freight` selects the initial view; `--capture=<absolute.png>` captures and exits. For map review, run Godot with `--path init --script <absolute path>/art/models/freight_train/capture_map.gd -- --no-log`; `--keep-open` retains that review session. Import first with `--headless --path init --import`.
