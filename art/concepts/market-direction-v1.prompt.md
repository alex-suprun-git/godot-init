Show the complete market as the main subject, a roughly compact rectangle beside the LEFT end of the station, close to its forecourt, both along the same railway axis. Market much shorter than screenshot 1, conceptually about one third its former length, no elongated empty paved strip. Station on right, market left/centre, railway corridor behind/below them, ordinary access road and sidewalk along front. Entrance to station remains clear and connected to market. Use a close elevated map view showing all market edges and enough of station and tracks to understand placement. No perspective horizon.
Approximately 24-32 small open clothes stalls in compact groups, neighboring roofs close, organised in several short rows. Continuous narrow 1.8-2.5 metre pedestrian shopping aisles, one readable cross aisle, people visibly walking between stands and toward station. Canvas roofs in visibly varied faded red, blue, teal, green, ochre and cream, some striped tarps; colourful as a group even at map zoom. Simple metal frames, folding counters, hanging jeans and jackets, striped traders' bags. Slight variations in roof heights and stall orientation but plausible modular footprint for eventual separate 3D models. Dense lively market, not festival food court. Worn concrete slabs, modest use wear, no ruin or rubbish dump. About 20 small shoppers/traders to demonstrate scale without hiding circulation. Late August 1997, mostly green foliage, warm soft afternoon light, restrained realistic painted game art, clean readable silhouettes and soft contact shadows. No new monumental architecture, market hall, new shop buildings, fountains, banners, readable text, logos, UI, labels, arrows, inset panels, collage or watermark. This is a feasible map concept, not a cinematic street-level illustration.
