# Boris Savvich Shumilov (district head)

Status: Draft v0 (voice and secrets written by the narrative partner; the look comes from the approved portrait where one exists). The look is defined once, in `art/PROMPTS.md`; this sheet only points to it.

## Name

**Шумилов Борис Саввич.** Everyone says «Борис Саввич» to his face; behind his back the staff say «Савич». The previous placeholder name was Pyotr Petrovich; the data file `init/scripts/data/week1.gd` and the HUD caption were updated to the new name on 2026-11-07.

## Role and scenes

The protagonist's boss: a tired cynic, once idealistic, now holding on until retirement and covering himself. Not evil: "that's how things work". His human side shows gradually, which makes removing him later more dramatic.

Scenes: `mon-1-arrival.md` (the door), `mon-2-boss-and-block5.md` (the briefing), `thu-1-gusev-day-and-inspection.md` (the inspection announced), `fri-2-restaurant.md` (the report). He says «ты» to the protagonist.

## Look

Approved portrait: `init/assets/portraits/boss.png`. Canonical look: `art/PROMPTS.md`, "Established looks" (heavy man in his early sixties, grey swept-back hair, heavy tired eyes, oversized grey suit, loosened burgundy striped tie).

## Voice

Heavy, unhurried, sardonic. Never raises his voice. Says «ты», names things plainly and leaves the unsaid unsaid.
Habits: asks a question and answers it himself («Вопросы есть? Нет. Вот и хорошо.»); makes people close the door.
Sample: «Спрашивать будут с меня. А я буду спрашивать с тебя.» / «Не всех слушай, но и не отмахивайся.»

## Wants

Quiet, a clean report, to hold on until retirement.

## Hides

A kickback on the fuel-oil contract; his dealings with the criminals through Sedov.

## How the bond grows

Знакомство: he judges by the Friday report.
Доверие: if the protagonist covers for him (the inspection), he takes him to dinner.
Долг: he now owes the protagonist, which is the most dangerous thing a man like him can feel.

## Assumptions

- His line about the House of Culture («говорить не обязан») is a test, not an instruction.
