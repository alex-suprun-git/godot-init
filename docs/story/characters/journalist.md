# The journalist (Vera Lisitsyna): character sheet and visual brief

Status: Draft v0. Name, paper and age are assumptions made by the narrative partner (see the end); the user may veto them. Scenes: `docs/story/week1/mon-3-dormitory.md`, `tue-1-vera-at-work.md`, `fri-1-inspection.md`.

## Who she is

- **Vera Lisitsyna**, 25, local. Born in the district, studied journalism by correspondence, lives in the dormitory on the protagonist's floor in a room she shares with a friend who is rarely there.
- Writes for the regional paper **«Речные вести»** (the river paper), district desk. Underpaid, overworked, stubborn. Her editor wants safe news; she wants true news.
- She is the only person in the dormitory who reads the whole paper, including the advertisements.
- Romance is a possible slow thread, never a promise. Her professional interest and the protagonist's job pull against each other: she needs sources inside the administration, and he cannot afford to be one.

## Voice

Quick, curious, a little tired. Asks a lot of questions, corrects herself mid-sentence, uses «ну» and «как бы»; less ironic than curious. Sample: «Вы к Сергеичу, на третью койку? Ну, держитесь, он ночью такое выводит, что я вообще не понимаю, как стены стоят.» She says what she does for a living last: «я всё больше по буквам».

## What she wants and hides

- Wants: a story that is true and printable, and someone in the administration who tells her the truth once.
- Hides: that she is a journalist (on Monday evening), and that she already has an old story about the boiler repairs that nobody would print.

## Two looks, two places

The same person must read as the same person in both, with a clear change of register. Both are needed in two formats each: a **conversation portrait** (half or three-quarter length, transparent background, for visual-novel scenes) and a **map token** photo (small passport-style, for the map).

| | **At home, in the dormitory** (Monday evening) | **At work, as press** (Tuesday morning, Friday noon) |
|---|---|---|
| Clothes | Oversized knitted cardigan over a plain T-shirt, loose trousers, wool socks or worn slippers | Dark graphite blazer, second-hand but ironed, white blouse, a burgundy lanyard with a press card |
| Hair | Loose bun held with a pencil | Pulled back in a neat ponytail |
| Props | A mug, a paperback with a cracked spine, a kettle | A steno notebook, a pen, a small dictaphone in the pocket, a canvas bag |
| Light and mood | Warm evening kitchen light, tired and unguarded | Cool morning daylight, alert and composed |
| Expression | Open, curious, half a smile | Focused, one eyebrow slightly raised |

## Visual reference direction

- Rendering: the project's painterly oil window-art manner, exactly like the existing portraits in `init/assets/portraits/` (see the window style block in `art/PROMPTS.md`). Late-1990s provincial regional-press people serve only as a reference for clothes, posture and props, not as a photographic look.
- Colours should sit with the project's interface language ("Living Chancellery": cream paper, warm graphite, burgundy ink). The burgundy lanyard is the one deliberate accent that links her to it.
- No real person's likeness; the face is an original design. Age 25, ordinary, memorable by expression rather than beauty.
- Generation prompts: `art/PROMPTS.md`, entry W9 (proposal, not generated yet). Follow `art/ASSET_PIPELINE.md` for generated raster art (original preserved outside `init/`, visual approval, optimization as a separate step). Existing portraits for style and framing: `init/assets/portraits/`.

## Assumptions

- Name (Vera Lisitsyna), age (25), paper («Речные вести») and the shared room are my choices.
- She lives on the protagonist's floor, so the Monday meeting happens in the corridor kitchen.
- "I'm more about letters" is the hint that she writes; the player should not connect it on Monday.
