# Mikhalych (fitter)

Status: Draft v0 (voice and secrets written by the narrative partner; the look comes from the approved portrait where one exists). The look is defined once, in `art/PROMPTS.md`; this sheet only points to it.

## Role and scenes

The district's fitter with golden hands, missing on Monday and found on Tuesday. The first assignable contact. His shame and his last autumn are the human core of the inspection thread.

Scenes: `tue-2-mikhalych.md`, the act in `thu-1-gusev-day-and-inspection.md`, the confession in `fri-1-inspection.md`. Requests: `hot_water`, the inspection chain part 4.

## Look

Approved portrait: `init/assets/portraits/mikhalych.png`. Canonical look: `art/PROMPTS.md`, "Established looks" (heavy-set man around sixty, grey hair under a flat cap, grey walrus moustache, ruddy face, padded work jacket over a striped undershirt, red pipe wrench).

## Voice

Quiet, ashamed, a little dry self-mockery. Broken sentences with «это...», «ну...», trailing off; speaks to his hands more than to people.
Habits: puts his palms flat on the table as if they were not his; repeats the end of a sentence under his breath.
Sample: «Ко мне осенью приходили, двое, такие вежливые, чаю попили, ничего не сказали, вот это хуже всего и было, что ничего.»

## Wants

Not to be asked about last autumn. To be useful again.

## Hides

Last autumn he signed a completion act for boiler repairs that were never done, under pressure; two polite men visited him afterwards and did nothing but explain what not to say. He is hungover and ashamed, not a drunk by nature. (Provisional variant A.)

## How the bond grows

Знакомство: he comes if he is asked, not ordered (Nina's advice).
Доверие (bond 1 or more): he says the polite men visited; by Thursday he can confess the act.
Долг: he will stand up in front of the engineer and say it was he who signed (`act_declared`), which costs the protagonist the boss's goodwill.

## Assumptions

- Age follows the portrait (around sixty).
- The act is a document, not a crime scene: violence stays offstage.
