# Gureev (fixer)

Status: Draft v0 (voice and secrets written by the narrative partner; the look comes from the approved portrait where one exists). The look is defined once, in `art/PROMPTS.md`; this sheet only points to it.

## Role and scenes

A quiet, reasonable man who works with the criminals, not for them, and is called in when a problem needs force. He is the mirror of the protagonist: both live by talking, he is a step further. He appears at the restaurant on Friday and plants the translator offer.

Scenes: `fri-2-restaurant.md`. Later: the side job from week 2.

## Look

No approved portrait yet. Proposal: `art/PROMPTS.md`, W10 (not generated). In short: about forty-five, lean, a grey-blue suit that looks expensive and cheap at once, no jewellery except a plain ring, a pen in the breast pocket, attentive eyes, a faint half smile; he looks like an accountant, not a gangster, which is deliberate against Gusev's flash.

## Voice

Smooth, reasonable, polite, speaks the language of both sides. Never raises his voice. Repeats the other person's key word back as a question.
Habits: pours for everyone but the boss; watches the room while he talks.
Sample: «Решает вопросы. Звучит почти как профессия.» / «Пока ничего. Запомните, что я так сказал.»

## Wants

To stay useful to both sides.

## Hides

He is the boss's bridge to the criminals. Nobody says it aloud.

## How the bond grows

Знакомство: the dinner; he is interested in the protagonist's language, not his job.
Доверие: he offers paid translation work (week 2 and later).
Долг: he asks for something that cannot be refused politely.
