# Baba Nina

Status: Draft v0 (voice and secrets written by the narrative partner; the look comes from the approved portrait where one exists). The look is defined once, in `art/PROMPTS.md`; this sheet only points to it.

## Role and scenes

The district's voice of the people and the first person the protagonist meets in a conversation. Old resident and elder of Block 5, a tireless complainant who writes everything down. She tests newcomers before she helps and later becomes the main way to the residents.

Scenes: `mon-2-boss-and-block5.md`, `wed-2-station-and-meeting.md` (the meeting), `thu-1-gusev-day-and-inspection.md` (rallying the yard). Requests: `hot_water`, `residents_meeting`.

## Look

Approved portrait: `init/assets/portraits/baba_nina.png`. Canonical look: `art/PROMPTS.md`, "Established looks" (woman in her seventies, floral headscarf, glasses on a cord, brown knitted cardigan, floral dress, green notebook held to her chest).

## Voice

Sharp, suspicious, short punchy lines. A dry metaphor now and then. Calls the protagonist «молодой человек»; writes him down as «новый помощник» until she knows more.
Habits: opens the notebook at a bookmark before she answers; counts things aloud; refuses to be hurried.
Sample: «Принято к сведению. Как будто это не вода, а дань.» / «Не вам, я воде.»

## Wants

Hot water, and a person who comes to the yard and tells people the truth. Not the boss on television.

## Hides

Nothing; she writes it all down. If she dislikes someone, she says so aloud, which is her version of discretion.

## How the bond grows

Знакомство: she tests him (introduce yourself honestly and she softens).
Доверие (bond 2 or more): she gives information without being asked, calls the residents together, and the player may send her to a meeting in their place.
Долг: she will vouch for the protagonist to the residents even when standing with them is low (the personal bond covers for the public one).

## Assumptions

- Her age (seventies), her notebook and her manner follow the portrait.
- She never learns the protagonist's name in week 1.
