# Tolik (driver)

Status: Draft v0 (voice and secrets written by the narrative partner; the look comes from the approved portrait where one exists). The look is defined once, in `art/PROMPTS.md`; this sheet only points to it.

## Role and scenes

The administration's driver. In week 1 he is the cheerful guide of the arrival drive (introduction only), then returns in week 2 as the driver for the coal action. He knows more than he says, and the break in his cheer at the restaurant is the point of the opening.

Scenes: `mon-1-arrival.md`, the ride home in `fri-2-restaurant.md`. Disabled as a contact until week 2.

## Look

Age: about 35, as the portrait reads (user decision; `art/PROMPTS.md` still says "forties", which the portrait overrides). Career: about ten years behind a wheel, first a long-haul driver to the border, then the administration; Boris Savvich is his second boss. A wife (Lyuda, trades at the market) and a daughter in the fourth class.

Approved portrait: `init/assets/portraits/tolik.png`. Canonical look: `art/PROMPTS.md`, "Established looks" (lean, gaunt man in his forties, messy dark hair, light stubble, sly sideways glance, cigarette behind his ear, worn brown leather jacket, car keys).

## Voice

Cheerful, talkative, warm, mocks himself, loves his own old Volga, which he keeps at home and drives only for his own errands; the boss's black foreign sedan is the official car he drives at work. Long flowing sentences and small jokes. Goes quiet on the one subject he must not discuss.
Habits: points at everything with the hand that is not on the wheel; waves at windows without looking at the road.
Sample: «Вот вокзал, а рядом рынок, тут весь район кормится, вы только кошелёк держите поближе, а то он у нас быстро находит нового хозяина.»

## Wants

To be liked and to keep the official car and his own Volga running; no trouble.

## Hides

He knows who eats at the restaurant. He stops talking, switches the radio off and speeds the cheer up again afterwards.

## How the bond grows

Знакомство: the drive, which ends at the boss's door.
Доверие (week 2 and later): he gives lifts when the official car is free, and tells what he saw from the driver's seat.
Долг: he will risk his job once, for the protagonist.

## Assumptions

- He is not a contact in week 1 and has no request.
- During the arrival drive he asks questions and answers for the silent protagonist, then carries on; the answers never state anything about the protagonist.
- The wife, the daughter and the father who is hard of hearing are the narrative partner's invention for the drive (not yet confirmed by the user).
