# Gusev (businessman)

Status: Draft v0 (voice and secrets written by the narrative partner; the look comes from the approved portrait where one exists). The look is defined once, in `art/PROMPTS.md`; this sheet only points to it.

## Role and scenes

A new-style businessman and boiler contractor (the firm «Стройремонт»). In week 1 he introduces himself without an envelope, asks for the wasteland kiosks and the contractor job on the same day, and is the third route to the secret of the false repair. He becomes the protagonist's first business contact and a creditor of favours.

Scenes: `tue-3-gusev-and-evening.md`, `thu-1-gusev-day-and-inspection.md`. Requests: `contractor`, `wasteland_stalls`.

## Look

Approved portrait: `init/assets/portraits/gusev.png` (see `art/PROMPTS.md`, W3 and "Established looks": a man in his late thirties, dark wavy hair, light stubble, a smug sideways grin, burgundy double-breasted jacket over an open white shirt, a loosened floral silk tie and pocket square, a gold chain and bracelet, a brick-sized mobile phone held to his ear).

## Voice

Friendly, expansive, generous with promises. Warm run-on sentences with asides («вы не подумайте»), pleased with himself, never an aphorism. Calls things «вопрос», never «проблема».
Habits: lets a pause hang until the other person fills it; hands over a card with his phone number.
Sample: «Меня Гусев зовут, фирма «Стройремонт», вы не подумайте, мне от вас ничего не надо, честное слово.»

## Wants

Contracts, the wasteland, a friend in the administration.

## Hides

Who backs him. The envelope scene is removed for now; the player does not learn about any backing in week 1.

## How the bond grows

Знакомство: he leaves without asking for anything, which is the point.
Доверие: he points at the rival firm and says what the player could not find out alone.
Долг: he calls in a favour exactly when the player has accepted both his offers.

## Assumptions

- His age follows the portrait (late thirties), not an older businessman.
- He is likeable on purpose; the cost is hidden in the favours.
