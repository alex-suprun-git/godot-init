# The protagonist

Status: Draft v0 (voice and secrets written by the narrative partner; the look comes from the approved portrait where one exists). The look is defined once, in `art/PROMPTS.md`; this sheet only points to it.

## Role and scenes

A young newcomer, the player's avatar: poor, bright, a clean slate for the boss. He is never named in text; other characters use nicknames and titles («молодой человек», «новый помощник», «Новенький», «ты»). The concrete biography is still open (see the root README, "Setting and Tone", and issue #16).

Present in every scene. Inner lines are dry and observant; he says things aloud only in conversations, as «ВЫ:».

## Look

Approved portrait: `init/assets/portraits/protagonist.png`. Canonical look: `art/PROMPTS.md`, "Established looks" (man of about 26, short brown hair, clean-shaven, tired watchful eyes, grey suit, white shirt, narrow dark striped tie, cardboard folders, a city view behind).

## Voice

Dry inner voice, observant, mildly ironic about himself and the district. No heroics; the game never judges him.
Habits: counts things that do not add up; notices what a person does with their hands.
Sample (inner): «Читать больше нечего. Читаете всё, вплоть до объявлений.»

## Wants

A way up, for someone (for whom is still open; the leitmotif is for whom you dirty your hands).

## Hides

Everything the player has not yet chosen: his name, his biography, how far he will go.

## How the bond grows

Fixed traits: translator of the neighbouring country's language; poor, in a shared dormitory room; a sister studying in the neighbour's capital whom he supports with money; hired for his clean slate.
Three trainable skills (documents, street, speech); no hidden numbers shown to the player.

## Assumptions

- Where he learned the language is open (a family link through the sister is possible).
- His age (about 26) follows the portrait.
