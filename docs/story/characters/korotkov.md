## Role and scenes

The engineer of the city heating commission who inspects Block 5 on Friday. Dry, procedural, polite to the point of being impolite. He appears once in week 1 and returns only if the protagonist's choices make him.

Scenes: `fri-1-inspection.md`.

## Look

No approved portrait yet. Proposal: `art/PROMPTS.md`, W12 (not generated). In short: about fifty-five, narrow tired face, short grey hair, thin-rimmed glasses, a grey raincoat buttoned in all weather, a briefcase older than the protagonist.

## Voice

Dry, tired, procedural. Greets everyone in turn, even the dog. Asks one precise question at a time.
Habits: touches radiators with his palm, counts aloud; writes everything down and says so.
Sample: «Листа нет. Я не утверждаю, что его убрали вы, я лишь констатирую, что его нет.»

## Wants

A signed report, and not to be lied to.

## Hides

What he does with a forged act: he is obliged to write it down, and he will.

## How the bond grows

Знакомство: the inspection only.
Доверие: if the protagonist told the truth, he will remember who sat in the chair.
Долг: none in week 1.
